Who it's for

Anyone who has to put content on a screen at an event — without a technical crew. If you can drag a file, you can run Cuey.

✓

No PowerPoint needed

Videos and images go in as they are — MP4, MOV, JPG, PNG. No embedding video in a deck, no "media not found", no waiting for it to load on stage.

✓

Nothing to learn

The main screen shows what's live and what's next. One GO button. Everything else — loop, smooth transition, hold — is a switch next to the file.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

Drop

All your videos and slides into the list, in the order they should play. Reorder by dragging.

Plug in the projector

Click "Open Output" — the picture goes fullscreen on the second display. On the laptop you see what's live and what's next.

Press Space

Every press — the next cue. Cut or dissolve, loop or play once, exactly as you set next to each file.

What's inside

Simple on the outside, professional underneath. The tools a show operator would want — without the menus they don't need.

  • GO Space, Enter or the big button — next cue
  • PROGRAM A monitor that shows exactly what the projector shows, dissolves included — even when rehearsing at home
  • NEXT Always see what's coming up
  • Cut / Dissolve A transition per cue, with its length in seconds
  • Loop A video or slide that runs until you press
  • Standby The video comes up on its first frame — one more GO plays it when the speaker says so
  • IN / OUT Trim a video without an editor — just drag a marker
  • Fit / Fill / Stretch How each picture fills the screen
  • Audio Volume per video, and a soundtrack for a still slide
  • AUTO Background loop: slides advance by themselves, videos play through, back to the start
  • Export MP4 Turn the loop into one video file that plays anywhere
  • BLACK / FREEZE Emergency buttons, always within reach
  • Countdown The last 5 seconds of a video, counted down — on your screen only, never on the projector
  • Save Save the whole show to a file and open it at the event
