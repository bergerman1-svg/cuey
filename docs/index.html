How it works

Three steps. Five minutes before the event is enough.

Drop your files in

Videos, pictures, music, PDF and PowerPoint — or a whole folder. Export from Canva or CapCut, drag into Cuey, reorder by dragging.

Plug in the screen

Connect the event screen — an LED wall or a TV. Cuey opens the Output window on it, full screen, by itself.

Press GO

Space advances. B is black. ← goes back. That's all anyone needs to know.
