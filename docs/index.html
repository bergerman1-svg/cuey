How it works

Three steps. Five minutes before the event is enough.

Drop your files in

Videos (MP4, MOV, WEBM) and slides (JPG, PNG). Export from Canva or CapCut, drag into Cuey, reorder by dragging.

Plug in the screen

Connect the projector or TV. Cuey opens the Output window on it, full screen, by itself.

Press GO

Space advances. B is black. ← goes back. That's all anyone needs to know.
