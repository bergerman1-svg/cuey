Getting started

  • In the browser: open cuey.live/app — nothing to install.
  • On your computer: download Cuey for Mac or Windows from cuey.live.
  • Your files stay with you: videos, pictures, music and shows are kept on your computer.

Add from Canva

  • Connect: sign in to Cuey, press + Add ▸ Canva ▸ Connect Canva, and allow Cuey in the Canva window. Keep the Cuey window open while you connect.
  • Bring a design in: pick a design, then Bring in as slides (each page becomes a cue) or As a video (MP4).
  • "This design has paid Canva elements": Canva doesn't export designs with paid elements you haven't licensed. Replace those elements in Canva, or use a Canva plan that includes them.
  • Disconnect: press Disconnect Canva in the same window. You can also remove Cuey in your Canva settings. Cuey only reads your designs and never changes them.
