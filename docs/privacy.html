Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cuey ("Cuey", "we") is software for playing slides, videos and music at live events. It is operated by Avi Berger Productions, Israel. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

In short

  • Your files stay with you. Videos, pictures, music and shows live on your computer. We don't upload them to our server.
  • An account is just an email. To sign in we need an email address, nothing more.
  • No ads, and we don't sell data.
  • AI Builder sends the programme you choose to an AI service to build slides from it. We don't keep the programme.
  • Add from Canva (only if you connect Canva): we keep only the link to your Canva account, never your designs.

1. What stays on your computer only

  • Shows and files: your cue list, media files, settings and recent shows. They are kept on your computer, in a .cuey file or in the browser's or app's local storage, and are not sent to us.
  • Local browser storage: language, volume, sign-in state and small preferences. We use it only to make the app work, not to track you.

2. Your account

  • What we keep: when you sign in, we keep your email address, a user ID, sign-in times, and whether you have Studio or Pro.
  • Your sign-in code: sent to your email. There are no passwords.
  • Why we need it: to recognise you, unlock what you bought, and send service messages such as your sign-in code.

3. AI Builder

  • What is sent: when you press Build, the programme text, and its picture if you uploaded one, are sent to our server. From there they go to Anthropic's Claude, which builds the running order of slides.
  • What we keep: only a usage record, without the content itself: when you built, how many characters, how many cues were made, and whether it succeeded. We use it for the daily limit.
  • At Anthropic: the content is handled under Anthropic's terms and policies for its API. Don't put sensitive information in a programme you don't want to send.

4. Add from Canva

  • What happens: if you connect Canva, Cuey can see the list of your designs and export a design you choose. Cuey only reads your designs; it never changes or deletes anything in Canva.
  • What we keep: only the link to your Canva account (access tokens), on our server, tied to your Cuey account. It never goes to your browser. We don't keep copies of your designs: the pages or video you choose are downloaded to your computer and saved with your show.
  • Stop it any time: "Disconnect Canva" in Cuey removes the link at Canva and deletes it from our server right away. You can also remove Cuey in your Canva settings.

5. Services we rely on

Service Why What it receives
Supabase server, accounts and database email, user ID, usage records
Resend sending your sign-in code your email address
Anthropic (Claude) AI Builder the programme text and picture
Canva Add from Canva (only if you connect it) requests for your design list and the designs you choose to export
Pixabay free backgrounds search words (through our server, without identifying details)
YouTube / Vimeo online cues you add their player loads on your device, under their policies
ntfy.sh, Google's STUN server screen sharing connection messages between devices. The video itself goes directly between the devices
GitHub hosting the site and downloads standard web logs (such as IP address)
Google Fonts, cdnjs fonts and code libraries standard web logs

Some of these services run on servers outside Israel, for example in the US or the EU.

6. Analytics and cookies

  • Tracking cookies and pixels: we don't use them.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous and cookie-free, through GoatCounter. We count page visits and a few actions in the app (for example "opened the app", "pressed GO for the first time") — never your files, names, or the content of your shows, and never anything that identifies you. You can switch it off in the app: profile menu ▸ Anonymous usage stats ▸ Off.

7. Emails

  • Service emails: always sent when needed, such as your sign-in code or a message about your purchase.
  • Marketing emails: only if you clearly agreed to them. Every one will have an unsubscribe link.

8. Payments

When we turn on payments, they will be handled by an outside payment provider. We never see or store your card details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase went through.

9. How long we keep things

  • Account details: while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • AI Builder usage records: up to 12 months.
  • Pixabay search results: up to 24 hours.
  • Canva link: until you disconnect Canva or delete your account. An unfinished connection: up to 1 hour.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, or delete your account. Write to privacy@cuey.live and we'll handle it within 30 days. If you are in the EU, you also have your rights under the GDPR.

11. Children

Cuey is made for people who organise and run events. It is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

12. Security

  • How we protect it: we use encrypted connections, limited access permissions and established providers.
  • What isn't perfect yet: no method is perfect. Cuey is in beta and we keep improving it.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date here. We'll tell you about any important change in the app or by email.
